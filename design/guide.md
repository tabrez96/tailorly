# Tailorly: guide

## Getting started
1. Open your shop's Tailorly web address on your phone (or a computer at the counter) and sign in with your phone number and password. (S01)
2. On Today, a short list shows the three things to set up first; you can do them in any order. Start with Add your employee: their name, phone number, daily wage and overtime rate. Their phone number and the password you set are their sign-in. (S02, S10)
3. Add your products: the garments you make or sell, with their usual price, the measurements they need and options such as Lining or Sleeve style. Under Materials, add what you use on garments, such as lining, can-can or hooks, with a unit, a cost price and a selling price. (S15)
4. Add your first customer and save their measurements for each garment they order. (S06, S07)
5. Give your employee the web address, their phone number and their password. When they sign in they go straight to their own work. (S01, S08)
6. Optional: set your rough monthly running costs (rent, electricity, thread) once, so every order shows what it cost you and what you gained. (S18, S11)

## How to use it
**Take an order and get paid**
1. Tap + New order on Orders, or press and hold the Orders tab. (S03)
2. Choose the customer, or add a new one. (S05)
3. Tap + Add item and pick the garment: the price fills in. Answer its options, check the measurements (copied from the customer), and add any materials, notes and photos. Tap Add to order. Repeat for each garment. (S20)
4. Set the due date, choose who makes it, enter any advance and tap Create order. (S05)
5. The order opens. When the customer pays, tap Record payment; the balance updates and the invoice shows Paid at zero. (S04)
6. When the work is done, tap Mark ready. Tick Send invoice on WhatsApp to send the customer their bill. When they collect, tap Mark delivered. (S04)

**Plan the work and time each garment**
1. Open the Production tab to see who is working on what right now and which orders are waiting. (S12)
2. Tap Assign on a waiting order: choose who makes it, check the time it should take and where it goes in their queue. The queue is sorted by due date for you; drag a garment, or tap its handle and use Up and Down, to change the order. (S16)
3. Your employee signs in, taps Start my day, and taps Switch on a garment to start its timer. Tap the photo to see its measurements and notes. (S08)
4. They tap Pause for a break, Switch to move to another garment, Finish item when it is done, and End my day before they leave. (S08)
5. Open a finished garment to compare the time it took with your estimate. (S17)

**Pay your staff each week**
1. On Today, tap the Staff pay card. (S02)
2. Check each day's start and end. Tap a day to fix it, or to add a day that was missed. Days with no record are unpaid days off; time past 8 hours is overtime. (S10)
3. Tap Record payout, check the amount, date and method, and tap Save. (S10)
4. Your employee sees the payment, and how it was worked out, on My pay. (S09)
