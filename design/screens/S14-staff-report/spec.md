## Content and actions
- Header: Back, overline Reports, title Staff. Reached from Staff report in S18 Settings and the report row.
- Report row, Staff selected. Period: Week, Month (default), previous and next.
- Headline: pay earned in the period, what was paid and when, what is due on the next pay day.
- Per employee card: days worked, days off (unpaid), hours present, overtime, time logged on garments (and share of time present), day pay, overtime pay (hours x hourly rate x multiplier), earned.
- Overtime by day: date, start and end, overtime; All days opens the full list. Payouts themselves are recorded in S10.

## States
- Main: this month (screen.html).
- No days recorded in the period: "No days recorded for Ali in this period."
- Loading: grey card outlines.
- Dark theme (states/dark.html).

## Notes and open questions
- Uses the same rules as S10: day pay for any day with a start and end, overtime past 8 hours.
- With one employee there is one card; more employees add one card each.
