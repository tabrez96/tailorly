# Tailorly: UX spec

## Users and primary jobs
- **Owner** (phone first, sometimes a laptop at the counter):
  1. Take an order: customer, then each item (garment) with its own measurements, notes and photos, then advance (S05, S20, S07).
  2. See who is working on what right now, and assign work (S12), then set the order it is made in: a suggested queue per employee that can be changed by hand, with an estimate of how long each item will take (S16, S05).
  3. Keep track of orders and money owed: status, each item's materials, payments, balance (S02, S03, S04, S19).
  3a. Log alterations and repairs after delivery and see what they cost the shop (S04, S11).
  4. Keep the product list: preset garments with their price and options, such as Lining (S15).
  5. Run weekly pay: check days and overtime, record the payout (S10), and see sales, profit, production, payments and staff reports (S11 to S14); open any garment's production record to compare time taken with the estimate (S17).
- Mockups use Sabreen for the owner and Ali (Ali Khan) for the employee.
- **Employee** (phone, often with busy hands):
  1. Start and end the day (S08).
  2. Time each garment: start, pause, switch, finish (S08), with the item's photos, measurements and notes at hand.
  3. See pay this week and past payments (S09).

## Information architecture
**Screen order** (how the screens are read and shown, owner first, then the employee; IDs stay as they are because tickets and specs refer to them):
1. Both: S01 Sign in.
2. Owner, Today: S02 Today.
3. Owner, Orders: S03 Orders, S05 New order, S20 Add item, S04 Order detail, S19 Item detail.
4. Owner, Customers: S06 Customers, S07 Customer detail.
5. Owner, Products: S15 Products.
6. Owner, Production: S12 Production (the Production tab), S16 Work plan, S17 Production record.
7. Owner, Settings: S18 Settings, S11 Reports (Sales and Profit), S13 Payments, S14 Staff report, S10 Staff, S21 AI assistants.
8. Employee: S08 My work, S09 My pay.

- S01 Sign in (both roles; the account decides where you land).
- Owner, bottom navigation (daily work only): **Today** S02, **Orders** S03, **Customers** S06, **Products** S15, **Production** S12. No separate New button; a short tap always just opens the tab. The employee navigation has no hold actions.
  - **Press and hold a tab** (half a second) to make something new: Orders opens S05 New order; Production opens S16 Work plan with the assign sheet (S16 states/assign.html) for the waiting order with the earliest due date, or S16 alone when every order is assigned; Customers opens the add customer form on S06; Products opens an empty product sheet on S15. Today has no hold action. The phone gives a short vibration and the tab gets a saffron ring as the hold lands. A one-time tip explains it after sign-in (S02 states/hold-tip.html).
  - Every hold shortcut also has a visible button, because holding can't be found by looking and isn't available to keyboard or screen-reader users: + New order on S03 (and New order on S07), Assign on S12 and S16, + Add on S06 and S15.
  - S15 Products is a nav tab, and Manage products in the S20 product picker opens it too.
  - S12 Production is the Production tab, the live shop floor: who is working on what right now (the item, its timer against the estimate, Up next), and the orders waiting for someone, each with Assign. Work plan, Ali's queue, Give Ali work and Assign open S16. A This week so far row opens the production report: S12 states/from-reports.html, with a back button and the report row, also reached from Production in S18 Settings and the Production this week card on S02.
  - S16 Work plan is a sub-page of the Production tab with a back button to S12, and is also opened from the Work plan card on S02, Change on the Assigned to row in S04, the Work plan link under Assign to in S05, and holding the Production tab.
  - S17 Production record (from the Production row on each item card in S04, and the garment lists in S12). A sub-page with a back button.
  - Reports: opened from the Reports list in S18 Settings; one report row at the top of each switches between S11 Sales and Profit, S12 Production, S13 Payments and S14 Staff. Each has a back button (S12 also lives on the Production tab, without them).
  - S18 Settings (from the round Settings gear in the S02 header), owner only: Reports (S11 to S14, each row with a live figure), Shop (S10 Staff, Running costs), AI assistants (S21, the MCP server) and Account (change password, sign out). S10 is also one tap from the Staff pay card on S02.
  - S19 Item detail (from the chevron on each item card in S04): one order line on its own page, with its photos, options, measurements, notes, materials, production and gain. A sub-page with a back button.
  - S04 Order detail (from S02, S03, S07, S11, S13), S05 New order (from + New order on S03, holding the Orders tab, and S07), S20 Add item (from + Add item and each item row on S05; a sub-page with a back button that returns to S05), S07 Customer detail (from S04, S06, S11).
- Employee, bottom navigation: **My work** S08, **My pay** S09.
- Sheets (bottom sheets, not separate screens): record payment, add material and Mark ready (options to fill, Send invoice on WhatsApp) (S04), record payout (S10), item details (S08), add or edit product (S15). Forms: edit measurements (S07), add or edit employee (S10).

## Key flows
1. **New custom order**: hold the Orders tab (or + New order on S03), S05 choose customer (or add one), + Add item opens S20: pick a product (price fills in), answer its options (for example Lining yes or no, Sleeve style), check its measurements, add materials, notes and photos, Add to order, back on S05 with the item in the list (tap a row to change it on S20); due date, Assign to (the hint says where it lands in the queue), advance, Create order, lands on S04.
1a. **Free order** (self or family): S05 Who pays: Free. Prices become Usual price, the advance is hidden, Create free order. No invoice; S04 shows Cost to the shop instead (states/free.html). Counted in S11 Profit under Free work and in the S03 Free filter.
2. **Measurements**: S06 customer, S07 Measurements, Edit (S07 edit-measurements), Save. A new custom item copies the customer's saved measurements for that garment and shows them on that item in S20; each item keeps its own copy, shown inside the item's card on S04. Edit on an item in S04 (states/edit-measurements.html) changes that item's copy, and can also update the customer's saved set.
3. **Ready-made sale**: S05 choose customer, + Add item, S20 set to Ready-made (states/ready-made.html), product, price, Add to order, payment, Create order; saved as Delivered.
4. **Employee's day**: S01 sign in, S08 Start my day, each item shows its photo (tap it for photos, measurements and notes), Switch on an item (timer starts), Pause or Switch to another item, Finish item, End my day.
5. **Materials**: materials belong to an item, never to the whole order. On the item card in S04, on S20 while taking the order, or on S19, + Add material (S04 states/add-material.html): the sheet shows which item it is for, pick the material from the secondary products in S15, quantity, cost price and selling price fill in (editable), Add to the customer's bill (off: the garment price covers it), Save. Cost can be filled in later. Product options that use a material add a material line to that item: at order on S05, or when done in the Mark ready sheet on S04 (states/ready-options.html; Skip for now asks again at Mark delivered).
6. **Payment**: S04 Record payment, amount defaults to balance, Save; balance updates; at zero the invoice shows Paid.
7. **Order done**: S04 Mark ready (the sheet has Send invoice on WhatsApp, unticked; disabled until the customer has a phone number on S07, states/ready-no-phone.html), customer collects, Mark delivered (confirms if a balance remains).
7b. **Send the invoice later**: on a Ready or Delivered order, S04 invoice card, Send invoice: WhatsApp opens to the customer's number with the items, total, received and balance written out; press send there.
7a. **Rework after delivery**: the customer brings a garment back. S03 (Delivered) or S07, open the order on S04, + Add rework (states/add-rework.html): which item, why (Fit alteration, Our mistake, Customer change, Repair), what to do, who does it, estimate, due date and the materials used, added with the same material sheet as items (never billed). Save rework: it joins that person's queue (S16) and they time it on S08 like any item (states/rework.html). S04 lists it under the items with its date, reason, minutes, materials and cost, and takes the total off the order's gain (states/rework-open.html; empty: states/no-rework.html). S11 Profit shows a Reworks line beside Free work.
8. **Weekly pay**: S02 Staff pay card or S18 Staff, S10 check the week, fix any day, Record payout, Save. The employee sees it in S09.
9. **Profit**: S11 Profit tab, by customer, sale or garment; add shop bills there. Set the running costs rate once (S11 states/running-costs.html); each order on S04 then shows its Cost and gain per item and for the whole order.
10. **Production report**: Production in S18 Settings, This week so far on the Production tab or the S02 card opens the S12 report (states/from-reports.html), Day, Week or Month: garments finished, by garment and by employee, on time and late; estimate vs time taken; tap Coming up to open S03, tap a garment to open S17.
10a. **Production record**: S04 item Production row, or a garment in S12, opens S17: time taken against the estimate and the usual time, who made it, start and finish, each work session, and an optional note on why it took longer.
11. **Payments**: S13, money received by method, what is still to collect, and who has owed longest (tap opens S04).
12. **Staff**: S14, days worked, days off, overtime, time logged on garments and pay earned for the week or month.
13. **Assign and plan**: the Production tab (S12) shows what each person is doing now and what is waiting. Assign on a waiting order, or Give Ali work on Ali's card (that person already chosen), opens the S16 assign sheet; choose Next up to make it the next thing they pick up. Or Work plan (S16), or the S02 Work plan card; or hold the Production tab on any owner screen to open the assign sheet for the next waiting order directly. Not assigned orders: Assign opens a sheet (states/assign.html): who makes it, your estimate to make it (pre-filled with the usual time) and where in the queue (Suggested or Next up). The same estimate field is on S05 under Assign to. The queue is sorted for you (rule below); drag the handle, or tap it and use Up and Down (states/reorder.html). Moved items keep their place and show Moved by you; Back to suggested undoes it. The employee works through the same order in S08 Up next.
14. **Products**: S03 Products, S15 list (Sold or Materials), + Add or tap a product, edit name, kind, type, price, measurement fields and options in the sheet (states/edit-product.html), Save. Materials: Materials, tap one, edit unit, cost price and selling price (states/edit-material.html). Changes apply to new orders only.

15. **Connect an AI assistant (MCP)**: S02 Settings gear, S18, AI assistants (S21), turn on the MCP server, choose what it can see and whether it may only read or also change, + New key, name it, Copy key (shown once, S21 states/new-key.html), paste the server address and key into the assistant. Revoke a key on S21 to cut that app off; Recent activity shows what each app did.

## Rules the screens follow
- Hourly rate = daily wage / 8. Overtime = time past 8 hours in a day, from day start and end, to the minute. Overtime pay = overtime hours x hourly rate x multiplier (default 1, set per employee in S10).
- Day pay = daily wage for any day with a start and end; no record means an unpaid day off. Week runs Monday to Sunday; pay day defaults to Saturday.
- An order is a header (customer, due date, assigned employee, status, invoice) with one or more items, the order lines. Each item is one garment: Custom or Ready-made, price, its own measurements (custom only), its own notes and photos, its own materials, its own status (Not started, In progress, Finished) and its own logged time. The employee times items, not orders. A blouse and a chudidar in one order are two items with two different measurement sets.
- An item's measurements are a copy of the customer's saved set for that garment, taken when the item is added; later changes to the customer's saved set do not change orders already made, unless saved from S04 with "Also update".
- Production counts a garment on the day its item is finished (Finish item on S08). On time = delivered on or before the due date. Late = past the due date and not Ready.
- Order status: Open, In progress (automatic when the first item timer starts), Ready, Delivered. Materials can be tagged to one item or to the whole order; costs always count at order level.
- The first photo of an item is its picture everywhere it appears (S04, S05, S20, S08). An item with no photo shows a camera placeholder.
- Products come in two kinds. **Primary** products are sold (garments, ready-made pieces). **Secondary** products are materials (lining, canvas, can-can, fall, hooks): a unit (m, pcs, pair, set), a cost price and a selling price per unit, and whether they are billed by default. Materials are only ever picked onto an item or a rework, never onto the order. A material line keeps a copy of its name, quantity, cost price and selling price. When it is on the bill, its selling price is a line under that item on the invoice and counts in the sale; otherwise only its cost counts.
- A primary product is a preset: name, type (Custom or Ready-made), usual price, its measurement fields (custom only) and options. A custom product's name picks the customer's measurement set for that garment. An option is Yes or no (Lining) or a Choice (Sleeve style: Short, Elbow, Full, Sleeveless); an option that adds material names the secondary product it uses (Lining uses Lining, cotton cream) and asks for the quantity and notes, at order or when done; cost and selling price come from the material. The order item keeps a copy of the product name, price and answers, so editing or removing a product never changes past orders. The price can be changed on each order.
- Option answers that use a material are material lines of that item and count in its gain.
- **Rework after delivery**: a separate job on one item of a Ready or Delivered order, with a reason, a note for the employee, an assignee, an estimate (45m until there are 3 finished reworks, then the average of the last 10), a due date and any materials used (picked from the secondary products, like on items). It is never invoiced: no charge, no payment, no change to the balance. Rework cost = logged minutes x hourly rate + its materials (no running-cost share); it comes off the item's and the order's gain. Its minutes count in the employee's pay, in S14 time logged and in the item's logged time, but S17 and S12 compare the estimate with the first make only, so the item stays Finished and its estimate comparison stays fair. The order stays Delivered and shows a Rework open chip until every rework is finished.
- One invoice per order. Payments have amount, date and method (Cash, UPI, Card, Bank). The invoice is sent as a WhatsApp message to the customer's phone number (from the Mark ready sheet or Send invoice on S04); the app remembers the date it was last sent. Free orders have no invoice to send.
- **Cost and gain** (S04, S11 by garment): an item's cost = staff time (minutes x hourly rate) + its materials + running costs (minutes x running cost per hour). Gain = price - cost, also shown as a share of the price. The order's gain is the sum of its items plus order-level materials. Until an item is finished its time is the estimate or the time logged, whichever is more, marked estimated. Ready-made items cost their materials only.
- **Running cost per hour** = the owner's rough monthly running costs (rent, electricity, thread and so on) / working hours a month (default 208), rounded to the rupee, set on S11 Profit. It spreads the bills over the work so each garment carries its share; it is an estimate for comparing price and cost, not accounting.
- Shop profit = sales - materials - all staff pay - shop bills actually recorded (no estimates).
- The employee never sees prices, balances or profit.
- Money is shown in INR with Indian grouping: ₹1,42,600. Dates as "Thu 12 Nov"; times as "9:02 am".

- **Suggested work order** (S16, S08 Up next): items already started come first; then the rest by due date, earliest first; on the same due date, the shorter job first. Time needed = the owner's estimate for the item. The estimate is pre-filled with the usual time: the average logged time of the last 10 finished items of that product, or 2 hours until there are 3. It can be changed until the item is started, then it is fixed, so S12 and S17 compare like with like. The employee does not see estimates. If a due date can't be met even in the best order, the item shows a Late risk chip. Items the owner moved stay where they were put; everything else re-sorts when orders are added, changed or finished. Assignment is per order: all its items go to one person's queue.
- **Free orders**: an order is paid by the Customer (default) or Free (self or family). A free order keeps the usual price for reference, has no invoice and no payments, and its cost to the shop = materials + staff time (logged minutes x hourly rate) + running costs. Reports show the count, the usual price not charged and the cost to the shop. Free orders add no sales, and their costs stay inside the shop's materials and staff pay.
- **MCP server**: off until the owner turns it on. It works only with an access key made on S21; each connected app has its own key, shown once. What it can see (orders and customers, measurements and photos, invoices and payments, staff and pay) and what it can do (Only read, or Read and change) are set once for all keys; defaults are read only with staff and pay hidden. It can never delete anything or record a payout. Every call is listed in S18 Recent activity.

## Design system
- **Themes**: light and dark. The app follows the phone or computer setting (`prefers-color-scheme`); there is no switch in the first version. Every colour is a named token, and screens use only tokens, so both themes come from one stylesheet (`:root` for light, `[data-theme=dark]` and the media query for dark). Each screen has a dark render in `states/dark.html`.
- **Colours** (token: light / dark)
  - Mulberry, the primary (buttons, active nav, links, chips, hero cards): `--pri` `#8A2E62` / `#E59AC4`; `--pri-d` `#6B2149` / `#F0B8D6`; `--pri-l` (tonal buttons, chips, avatars, active nav pill, bar tracks) `#F6E8F0` / `#3B2131`; text on primary `--on-pri` `#FFFFFF` / `#2A0F20`.
  - Hero card `--hero`: a diagonal mulberry gradient, light `#A23A74` to `#7A2856` to `#561A3C`, dark `#6A2950` to `#4A1D38` to `#311426`, with white text and secondary text `--hero-muted` `#F4DCEA` / `#E8C6D9` (4.8:1 on the lightest stop; white text 6.2:1). One per screen at most, for the live or key figure: Ali's day on S02, the timer on S08, this week's pay on S09, the headline figure on each report (S11 to S14). It is the only gradient in the app UI.
  - Saffron `--acc` `#F2B84B` in both themes: accent for the one action that matters most (Pause, Start my day), the press-and-hold ring on nav tabs, and focus rings. Always with dark text `#1C1B1A`.
  - Page `--bg` warm linen `#F6F2ED` / `#141012`; cards and sheets `--card` `#FFFFFF` / `#1F191C`; sunken areas (segmented control, measurement tiles, grey chips) `--sunk` `#EFEAE3` / `#2A2226`; dividers `--line` `#ECE5DB` / `#352C30`; card edge `--edge` `rgba(74,30,56,.06)` / `#352C30`; field border `--field` `#B9AFA2` / `#6E6267`.
  - Elevation: in light, cards float on a soft two-layer shadow (`0 1px 2px` and `0 8px 24px -6px`, mulberry-tinted at 5 to 9%) instead of hard borders; in dark there are no shadows, cards use a 1 px `--edge` border. Floating nav and sheets use a deeper shadow.
  - Text `--ink` `#1C1B1A` / `#F3EEEA`; muted `--muted` `#5F5850` / `#B3A9A4`. Disabled button `#E3DDD3` on `#6F675E` / `#2D2529` on `#9A8F94`.
  - Success `--ok` `#2F7D4F` on `#E6F3EA` / `#6FCF97` on `#173324` (Ready, Paid, profit). Warning `--warn` `#9A4A00` on `#FDF0DC` / `#F2B36B` on `#3A2812` (money due, Not started, overtime). Error `--bad` `#B42318` on `#FDECEA` / `#FF8A80` on `#3D1A18`.
  - Why mulberry: it is a silk and textile colour, warm and confident, pairs with saffron the way a sari border does, and is clearly different from the success green and error red, so status chips never look like buttons.
- **Type**: **Plus Jakarta Sans** (variable, weights 200 to 800, SIL Open Font License), self-hosted as woff2 with the app, no third-party font service. It is a modern geometric sans with open, friendly shapes, tabular figures and the ₹ sign. Fallback: system-ui, -apple-system, Segoe UI, Roboto. Scale: page title 30/800 (tracking -0.03em), hero number 52/800 (S08 timer), big number 28/800 tabular, sheet and empty-state title 22/800, section heading 17/800 sentence case, body 15/500, labels 13/700, small 13/500, overline (date above the title) 12/700 uppercase. Money and times use tabular figures. Mockups embed a subset of the font so the renders show the real type.
- **Spacing**: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24 px. Page padding 16. Radius: cards and lists 20, hero and timer cards 24, fields and measurement tiles 14, sheets 28 at the top, buttons, chips, segmented controls and nav items fully rounded (pill).
- **Components**: switch (52 x 32 pill, mulberry when on, sunken with a field-colour ring when off, role="switch", always next to a text label); checkbox row (24 px box, radius 8, field-colour ring, mulberry when ticked, in a 60 px row with a bold label and one muted line under it; disabled rows sit on `--sunk` and the muted line says what to do), used for one-off choices made with an action, such as Send invoice on WhatsApp; large-title header, the same on every screen (no coloured bar, on the page background; top to bottom: on sub-pages a round 44 px Back button with a chevron, on its own row; the optional overline above both title and action; then one row holding the 30 px title, which takes the free space on the left and wraps if it is long, and the screen's one tonal pill action at its natural width on the right, never full width and never on a row of its own: + New order on S03, + Add on S06, S10 and S15, Work plan on S12, Assign on S16, Edit on S04, S07 and S19); floating bottom navigation (68 px pill bar inset 12 px from the edges, five equal items, line icon plus label, active item in a `--pri-l` pill); card; hero card; list (one card with inset dividers, rows min 60 px); status chips (pill with a leading dot, the word is always shown); buttons, all pills, min 44 px (48 px default): primary (mulberry with a soft glow), tonal secondary (`--pri-l` with `--pri-d` text, replaces outline buttons), accent (saffron) and ghost; press and hold on nav tabs (shortcut to the tab's create form after 500 ms, with a short vibration and a 3 px saffron ring on the tab; a tap still just opens the tab; never the only way to an action); queue row (rank number, 52 px photo, garment and order, due date and time needed, status chip, 44 px drag handle; a picked-up row is tinted `--pri-l` with a 2 px mulberry ring and a bottom bar with Up, Down and Done); radio rows in sheets (60 px, ring border, checked row tinted); product row (garment or bag icon tile, name, type chip, options summary, price, chevron); option editor (sunken panel: name, Yes or no / Choice, what it asks for as toggle chips, At order / When done); fixed bottom action bar with rounded top on forms and details; segmented control (pill); filter chips (selected chip is solid ink); bottom sheet with grab handle; measurement tiles (value over label, 3 per row); status steps (rounded progress segments). Item card (one per order line: first photo 52 px, number pill, garment, type chip, price, status chip, then that item's measurement tiles, notes on a sunken panel and a photo strip; can fold to a one-line summary). Garment photo thumbnails: 52 px (lists), 76 px (cards, hero), full width 170 px (details sheet), radius 14 to 20, a small "1/3" count badge when there are more; no photo shows a camera icon on `--sunk`. Nav icons are 24 px line icons, 1.9 px stroke, round caps: Today (house), Orders (list sheet), Customers (two people), Products (hanger), Production (thread spool); in S18 Staff (ID badge), Reports (bars); My work (stopwatch), My pay (wallet).
- **Tone of voice**: plain, short, friendly, like the shop talks. "To collect", not "Accounts receivable". Errors say what to do next. Address the reader as "you"; name people ("End Ali's day").
- **Logo**: two flat lapels of a tailored jacket meeting in a V, on a rounded tile with a diagonal mulberry gradient (`#C2558F` top left to `#4E1838` bottom right). The left lapel is white, the right a saffron gradient (`#F7C766` to `#EE9A3A`); the two tones meet where a jacket closes. It says "tailored": the lapel is the part of a garment that shows it was made to fit. The shapes are flat and geometric, with nothing extending past the V, so at 16 px it still reads as a white and saffron V on mulberry. The lighter top of the tile keeps it visible on dark backgrounds, so the same mark is used in both themes. `brand/mark.svg` is the app icon and favicon and is used alone on S01 and in empty states; `brand/logo.svg` (mark plus "Tailorly" in deep mulberry `#6B2149`, set in Plus Jakarta Sans ExtraBold with tight tracking and converted to outlines) is for light, wide spaces such as the public page; on dark backgrounds set the name in `#F3EEEA`. Apart from the hero card, the gradients are for the logo only. Keep the tile and both lapel colours, don't add tools or tape, and don't put the mark on a busy background.

## Accessibility
- Text contrast at least 4.5:1 (muted text on linen is about 6:1; white on mulberry about 8:1). Both themes meet these ratios: in dark, muted text on the page is about 8:1, mulberry links on cards about 7.8:1, and button text on light mulberry about 8:1. Saffron is never used for text.
- Touch targets at least 44 x 44 px; the main employee buttons are full width.
- Press and hold on nav tabs is only a shortcut: every action it opens also has a visible button, and the tabs keep their normal names for screen readers.
- Reordering never needs dragging: tapping the handle picks the item up and Up, Down and Done move it; each move is announced ("Kurta moved to position 4 of 6").
- Every field has a visible label; errors are shown in text next to the field, not by colour alone, and an alert summary is announced (role="alert"). Focus goes to the first field with an error.
- Visible focus ring: 3 px saffron outline. Focus order follows reading order; sheets trap focus and return it to the button that opened them; Escape closes them.
- Status is always a word, not only a colour. The timer is announced politely, not every second.
- Photos have text alternatives (the item name and "photo 1 of 3").

## Open questions
- Rework: whether S12 Production should also show rework time per garment and per employee (for spotting which garments come back most), to be decided from use; for now it is on S11 Profit and each S17 record.
- Work plan assigns a whole order to one person. Splitting items of one order between two employees can come later if the shop grows.
- Profit attribution: per order and per item, staff time by logged minutes and shop bills by the estimated running cost per hour (S04 Cost and gain); the shop total uses real pay and bills. Unlogged pay counts only at shop level.
- MCP reports: costs and gains are readable through Invoices and payments on S21; whether assistants need a separate switch for them can be decided from use.
- Owner password recovery: no email in scope; proposed reset by whoever hosts the app.
- Week boundary and pay day (proposed Mon to Sun, paid Saturday).
- Advances against pay and public holidays: not handled in the first version.
- MCP: which assistants to connect first, and whether Read and change is needed in the first version. Access per key can come later.
- Further reports beyond Sales, Profit, Production, Payments and Staff (for example garments by fabric or repeat customers): to be chosen from use.
