# Tailorly

## The problem
Customers' measurements are in one notebook, orders in another, and who still owes you money is on scraps of paper. Your tailor's hours, overtime and weekly pay are worked out by hand, and you can never quite tell whether a garment made you money once the lining and the hours are counted.

## How it helps
- Keeps every customer's measurements, and each order's garments, photos and notes, in one place on your phone.
- Shows who is stitching what right now, what is due next, and lets your staff time each garment with a tap.
- Tracks payments and balances, and sends the bill to the customer on WhatsApp.
- Works out weekly pay and overtime from the days worked, and shows what each order really cost you.

## Who it's for
Small tailoring shops that make clothes to measure and sell some ready-made pieces, with an owner and a tailor or two.
